**12** <sup>1</sup> In that day you will say, “I will give thanks to you, the LORD; [^ref_1] for though you were angry with me, your anger has turned away and you comfort me. <sup>2</sup> Behold, God is my salvation. I will trust, and will not be afraid; for the LORD, [^ref_2] the LORD, [^ref_3] is my strength and song; and he has become my salvation.” <sup>3</sup> Therefore with joy you will draw water out of the wells of salvation. <sup>4</sup> In that day you will say, “Give thanks to the LORD! Call on his name! Declare his doings among the peoples! Proclaim that his name is exalted! <sup>5</sup> Sing to the LORD, for he has done excellent things! Let this be known in all the earth! <sup>6</sup> Cry aloud and shout, you inhabitant of Zion, for the Holy One of Israel is great among you!”
