*A Song of Ascents.*

> **121** <sup>1</sup> I will lift up my eyes to the hills.
> Where does my help come from?
> <sup>2</sup> My help comes from the LORD,
> who made heaven and earth.

> <sup>3</sup> He will not allow your foot to be moved.
> He who keeps you will not slumber.
> <sup>4</sup> Behold, he who keeps Israel
> will neither slumber nor sleep.
> <sup>5</sup> The LORD is your keeper.
> The LORD is your shade on your right hand.
> <sup>6</sup> The sun will not harm you by day,
> nor the moon by night.
> <sup>7</sup> The LORD will keep you from all evil.
> He will keep your soul.
> <sup>8</sup> The LORD will keep your going out and your coming in,
> from this time forward, and forever more.
