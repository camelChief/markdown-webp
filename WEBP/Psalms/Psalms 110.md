*A Psalm by David.*

> **110** <sup>1</sup> The LORD says to my Lord, “Sit at my right hand,
> until I make your enemies your footstool for your feet.”
> <sup>2</sup> The LORD will send out the rod of your strength out of Zion.
> Rule among your enemies.
> <sup>3</sup> Your people offer themselves willingly in the day of your power, in holy array.
> Out of the womb of the morning, you have the dew of your youth.
> <sup>4</sup> The LORD has sworn, and will not change his mind:
> “You are a priest forever in the order of Melchizedek.”
> <sup>5</sup> The Lord is at your right hand.
> He will crush kings in the day of his wrath.
> <sup>6</sup> He will judge among the nations.
> He will heap up dead bodies.
> He will crush the ruler of the whole earth.
> <sup>7</sup> He will drink of the brook on the way;
> therefore he will lift up his head.
