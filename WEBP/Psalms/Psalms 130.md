*A Song of Ascents.*

> **130** <sup>1</sup> Out of the depths I have cried to you, LORD. [^ref_1]
> <sup>2</sup> Lord, hear my voice.
> Let your ears be attentive to the voice of my petitions.
> <sup>3</sup> If you, LORD, [^ref_2] kept a record of sins,
> Lord, who could stand?
> <sup>4</sup> But there is forgiveness with you,
> therefore you are feared.
> <sup>5</sup> I wait for the LORD.
> My soul waits.
> I hope in his word.
> <sup>6</sup> My soul longs for the Lord more than watchmen long for the morning,
> more than watchmen for the morning.
> <sup>7</sup> Israel, hope in the LORD,
> for there is loving kindness with the LORD.
> Abundant redemption is with him.
> <sup>8</sup> He will redeem Israel from all their sins.
