> **93** <sup>1</sup> The LORD reigns!
> He is clothed with majesty!
> The LORD is armed with strength.
> The world also is established.
> It can’t be moved.
> <sup>2</sup> Your throne is established from long ago.
> You are from everlasting.
> <sup>3</sup> The floods have lifted up, LORD,
> the floods have lifted up their voice.
> The floods lift up their waves.
> <sup>4</sup> Above the voices of many waters,
> the mighty breakers of the sea,
> the LORD on high is mighty.
> <sup>5</sup> Your statutes stand firm.
> Holiness adorns your house,
> LORD, forever more.
