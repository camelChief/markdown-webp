*A Song of Ascents. By David.*

> **133** <sup>1</sup> See how good and how pleasant it is
> for brothers to live together in unity!
> <sup>2</sup> It is like the precious oil on the head,
> that ran down on the beard,
> even Aaron’s beard,
> that came down on the edge of his robes,
> <sup>3</sup> like the dew of Hermon,
> that comes down on the hills of Zion;
> for there the LORD gives the blessing,
> even life forever more.
