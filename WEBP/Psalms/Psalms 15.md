*A Psalm by David.*

> **15** <sup>1</sup> LORD, who shall dwell in your sanctuary?
> Who shall live on your holy hill?
> <sup>2</sup> He who walks blamelessly and does what is right,
> and speaks truth in his heart;
> <sup>3</sup> he who doesn’t slander with his tongue,
> nor does evil to his friend,
> nor casts slurs against his fellow man;
> <sup>4</sup> in whose eyes a vile man is despised,
> but who honors those who fear the LORD;
> he who keeps an oath even when it hurts, and doesn’t change;
> <sup>5</sup> he who doesn’t lend out his money for usury,
> nor take a bribe against the innocent.

> He who does these things shall never be shaken.
