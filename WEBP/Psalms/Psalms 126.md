*A Song of Ascents.*

> **126** <sup>1</sup> When the LORD brought back those who returned to Zion,
> we were like those who dream.
> <sup>2</sup> Then our mouth was filled with laughter,
> and our tongue with singing.
> Then they said among the nations,
> “The LORD has done great things for them.”
> <sup>3</sup> The LORD has done great things for us,
> and we are glad.
> <sup>4</sup> Restore our fortunes again, LORD,
> like the streams in the Negev.
> <sup>5</sup> Those who sow in tears will reap in joy.
> <sup>6</sup> He who goes out weeping, carrying seed for sowing,
> will certainly come again with joy, carrying his sheaves.
