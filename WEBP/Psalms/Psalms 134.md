*A Song of Ascents.*

> **134** <sup>1</sup> Look! Praise the LORD, all you servants of the LORD,
> who stand by night in the LORD’s house!
> <sup>2</sup> Lift up your hands in the sanctuary.
> Praise the LORD!
> <sup>3</sup> May the LORD bless you from Zion,
> even he who made heaven and earth.
