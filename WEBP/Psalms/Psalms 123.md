*A Song of Ascents.*

> **123** <sup>1</sup> I lift up my eyes to you,
> you who sit in the heavens.
> <sup>2</sup> Behold, as the eyes of servants look to the hand of their master,
> as the eyes of a maid to the hand of her mistress,
> so our eyes look to the LORD, our God,
> until he has mercy on us.
> <sup>3</sup> Have mercy on us, LORD, have mercy on us,
> for we have endured much contempt.
> <sup>4</sup> Our soul is exceedingly filled with the scoffing of those who are at ease,
> with the contempt of the proud.
